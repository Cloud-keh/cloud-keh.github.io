NUMERICAL METHODS
Research project · Central South UniversitySince Sep. 2025

High-performance 3D Seismic Wave Simulation

Supervised by Prof. Kejia Pan

Studied frequency-domain spectral-element modeling of seismic waves in viscoelastic anisotropic media with irregular topography, focusing on sparse linear-system solvers and parallel efficiency.

  • Compared MUMPS, AGMG, BoomerAMG, and AMGCL; evaluated preconditioning, numerical libraries, and hybrid OpenMP/MPI configurations.
  • On the evaluated configurations, AGMG reduced serial runtime by 91.59% relative to MUMPS; the tuned parallel setup achieved approximately 13× speedup with over 90% lower memory use.
